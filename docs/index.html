Support & common questions

What is a one line a day journal?
A journal with a single sentence per day. The payoff arrives a year later, when today's page shows this exact date last year. It's the lowest-effort journaling habit that sticks.

Where is my journal stored?
Entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your journal, so export first if you're switching phones.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → OneLine → Cancel. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?
OneLine → Settings → Restore purchases (same Apple ID).
