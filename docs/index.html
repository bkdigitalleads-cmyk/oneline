OneLine Support

OneLine is a one-line-a-day journal for iPhone. Write one honest line about today; next year, read it back on this exact day.

Common questions

Where is my journal stored? Entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your journal, so export first if you're switching phones.

How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → OneLine → Cancel. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone? OneLine → Settings → Restore purchases (make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID).

I found a bug. Email us — we read everything.
